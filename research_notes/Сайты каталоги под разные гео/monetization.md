# Monetization methods for niche directory websites, with benchmarks by country

Research date: 2026-11-09. Method: WebSearch only. WebFetch was attempted once, on adbenchly.com, and failed (DNS blocked), so most figures come from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Most sources are vendor blogs or self-reported operator posts. Treat every figure as directional. Where a search summary did not clearly tie a figure to one URL, the note says "attribution approximate".

---

## 1. Featured/premium listings and subscriptions (prices, conversion, sales process, tools)

### Takeaway
Published prices for niche-directory featured or premium listings mostly fall between about $30 and $200/month. B2B and software directories reach $200-500/month, and a few well-known software directories charge about $197/month for featured slots. No source reports a measured free-to-paid conversion rate. Operator anecdotes suggest only "a few" businesses pay at first. The usual sales approaches are free listings that owners claim and then upgrade, plus cold outreach once the directory has some proof of traffic.

### Cited Findings
- One vendor guide gives these price ranges: general directories charge $10-30/month for featured listings, and niche directories can charge 3-5x more. — [UKBusinessForums thread / vendor guides in search set](https://www.ukbusinessforums.co.uk/threads/niche-directory-how-much-to-charge-for-a-premium-listing.280674/); [Jasmine Directory, "business directory listings what they cost in 2026"](https://www.jasminedirectory.com/blog/business-directory-listings-what-they-cost-in-2026-2/) (attribution approximate)
- OpenAlternative, an open-source software directory, prices featured listings at **$197/month**. Featured listings are said to bring in about **35% of its revenue** (self-reported by the operator, via a vendor blog). — [Dirstarter, "Directory revenue models" (Mar 2026)](https://dirstarter.com/blog/directory-revenue-models)
- Examples of tiered pricing: directory-software vendors put featured placement at **$79-99/month**. One regional directory publishes **$35/mo basic, $150/mo enhanced and $550/mo full sponsorship**. — [eDirectory webinar post](https://www.edirectory.com/updates/?p=4361); [Spreadsimple monetization guide](https://spreadsimple.com/blog/how-to-monetize-a-directory-website/) (attribution approximate)
- One secondary citation puts B2B niche directories at $200-500/month per business, with no method given. — [AI Magic Directory, monetization strategies](https://aimagicdirectory.pages.dev/blog/directory-monetization-strategies)
- Vendor pricing examples assume, for instance, "50 featured listings at $149/mo" with no evidence that this is achievable. A UK ROI example assumes a 50% conversion of enquiries for a niche service. That is a hypothetical input, not observed data. — [noticed.uk ROI calculation](https://noticed.uk/blog/can-a-premium-business-listing-pay-for-itself)
- An operator anecdote from a bar directory: he offered a $49/month subscription with better placement, blog features and social promos. "A few owners" took it, and he calculated that even 30 paying businesses would give only about $1,500 MRR. He moved to a marketplace model. Self-reported. — [Indie Hackers, "Turning my back on $1k a month directory website"](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/turning-my-back-on-1k-a-month-directory-website-to-chase-the-potential-of-1million-a-year-two-sided-marketplace-588c3ddc82)
- A local classifieds plus business directory site at **more than $25K MRR** reported this revenue mix: about 55% featured/bump listing fees, about 25% power-seller subscriptions, about 15% escrow/transaction fees and about 5% local business directory placements. The founder said cold outreach to local service businesses "converted better than expected once I had even 50 real users to point to as proof". Self-reported. — [Indie Hackers, "Boring local classifieds site crossed $25K MRR"](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/five-years-in-how-a-boring-local-classifieds-site-quietly-crossed-25k-mrr-4d2395fb81)
- One directory founder had a $17k month that came mostly from one-time submissions. A commenter warned that one-time submission revenue "is a wave you are riding, not an asset", and only recurring MRR compounds. — [Indie Hackers, "Stuck at $300/mo until revenue skyrocketed to $17k/mo"](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/stuck-at-300-mo-until-revenue-skyrocketed-to-17k-mo-in-a-month-uMOSolSKjXGX74a0lVGQ)
- A free basic listing with paid upgrades is described as the highest-volume model. — [Spreadsimple](https://blog.spreadsimple.com/blog/how-to-monetize-a-directory-website)
- Tools and costs:
  - Brilliant Directories costs about **$40-120/mo** according to Dirstarter (checked against vendor pages in June 2026), but Directify lists **$145-497/mo**. The sources conflict. — [Dirstarter, best directory software (Jul 2026)](https://dirstarter.com/blog/best-directory-software); [Directify, best directory builders](https://directify.app/blog/best-directory-website-builders/); [Capterra BD pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/133173/Brilliant-Directories/pricing/)
  - Directorist's popular plan costs about **$134/year** (2025 data). — [Whalesync, top directory builders 2025](https://www.whalesync.com/blog/top-10-directory-builders-in-2025)
  - GeoDirectory has a free core and paid add-ons at about **$49-79/year each**, with the pricing manager or monetization add-on among them. — [Directify](https://directify.app/blog/best-directory-website-builders/); [Whalesync](https://www.whalesync.com/blog/top-10-directory-builders-in-2025)
  - Directify supports configurable paid listing features. — [Directify, customizing paid listing features](https://directify.app/blog/customizing-paid-listing-features-on-directify/)

### Inferences
- A realistic planning range for a local-services niche directory is $29-99/month for a featured slot, and $99-299/month for B2B or software niches. The $197/month OpenAlternative figure is the best-documented point for software directories.
- The revenue ceiling for featured listings equals the number of paying businesses times the price. With low single-digit-percent uptake, which is implied by the anecdotes but not measured, a directory of about 1,000 listings might get roughly 10-30 payers.
- Recurring subscriptions are worth more at sale than one-time fees, because buyers value MRR. See section 5.

### Gaps
- I found no measured free-to-paid or claimed-to-paid conversion rate from any directory.
- No Stripe-specific benchmarks came up.
- I found no non-US (EU, LatAm or CIS) price points for featured listings. European, LatAm and CIS price levels are unknown from these sources.

## 2. Lead generation, pay-per-lead, pay-per-call, rank-and-rent

### Takeaway
What a lead is worth varies enormously by vertical. Locksmith leads run about $15-75, while water-damage restoration leads run up to about $2,250 (Service Direct ranges). Legal leads cost about $130 on Google search, and B2B legal lead generation reaches about $650 per lead. Pay-per-call payouts from vendor sources run about $14-258 for home services and $50-400 for legal. Rank-and-rent sites rent for about $100-2,000/month per trade and city, and most of those figures are sellers' asking prices.

### Cited Findings
- WordStream/LocaliQ 2025: the all-industry Google Ads cost per lead averaged about **$70.11**. Attorneys and legal services cost about **$131.63 per lead**, against a $66.69 all-industry average in one edition. — [Search Engine Land (2025 benchmarks)](https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-costs-rising-but-conversion-rates-improved-in-2025-477927); [emulent CPL by industry](https://emulent.com/resources/average-cost-per-lead-by-channel-and-industry) (the $131.63 attribution is approximate)
- Sopro's 2025 B2B report: legal services is the most expensive B2B lead-gen industry, at an average CPL of **about $650**. — [Sopro](https://sopro.io/?p=32664)
- B2B average CPL in 2025 was **about $200** (Zeliq, Dec 2025). LinkedIn CPLs are **$100-400+**. — [Zeliq](https://www.zeliq.com/blog/b2b-cost-per-lead); [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/cost-per-lead-benchmark)
- Facebook CPL from Focus Digital (Jul 2025): Home Services **$45.50**, Healthcare **$41.60**, B2B SaaS **$63.40**. — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-cost-per-lead-on-facebook-july-2025-report/)
- Google Local Services Ads: about **$53 blended cost per lead** (Feb 2026, SearchLight Digital benchmark as summarized by a third party). — summarized in [landerlab CPL by industry](https://landerlab.io/blog/cost-per-lead-by-industry) (attribution approximate)
- Ranges by trade: plumbing, electrical and HVAC cost **$15-40 per lead** (OptiMix estimate). Service Direct publishes **$15-75 per lead for locksmiths** and **$500-2,250 for water-damage restoration**. Healthcare and medical cost $30-80 per lead (OptiMix) or **$250-400** under pay-per-lead pricing (QualityUnit), so the figures depend on the pricing model. — [OptiMix 2026](https://optimix51-optimix-blog.hf.space/?p=2918); [QualityUnit, how much to pay per lead](https://cdn.qualityunit.com/faq/how-much-to-pay-per-lead); Service Direct ranges as cited in [adwave CPL by industry](https://adwave.com/resources/cost-per-lead-by-industry) (attribution approximate)
- Pay-per-call: one vendor gives legal payouts of about **$50-400 per call** and home services about **$14-258 per call**, with no method stated. Higher-payout offers come with stricter rules: minimum call duration, refund windows and quality review. — [Yepads pay-per-call guide](https://yepads.com/what-is-pay-per-call-affiliate-marketing-guide/); [QualityUnit, industries for pay-per-call](https://cdn.qualityunit.com/blog/industries-benefit-pay-per-call-affiliate-programs)
- Ringba's weekly pay-per-call reports (2021, old data) consistently ranked home services at or near the top, with legal and insurance close behind. — [Ringba, Pay Per Call Report Sep 2021](https://www.ringba.com/blog/the-pay-per-call-report-september-3-2021)
- Networks named in the results include Ringba (call-tracking platform), Service Direct, MutualCall, Lead Smart and WeCall. It is unclear whether the last two are still active. — [Ringba blog](https://www.ringba.com/blog/the-pay-per-call-report-march-19-2021); [affpaying MutualCall](https://www.affpaying.com/mutualcall)
- Rank-and-rent prices: one vendor's exclusive plumbing leads from rented sites start at **$99/month**. A guide says rent is "often a few hundred dollars per site". An agency charges from **$500/month per trade per service area**. One lead-gen guide suggests **$1,000-2,000/month**, which is a seller's suggestion, not market data. An alternative is per-verified-lead pricing at **$40-95**. — [il.ly rank and rent](https://il.ly/seo/rank-and-rent); [Prospeo, lead gen business model 2026](https://prospeo.io/s/lead-gen-business-model); [bold-coast-leads pricing](https://bold-coast-leads.pages.dev/pricing/); [orbit-marketplace](https://orbit-marketplace.pages.dev/how-rank-and-rent-works)

### Inferences
- What a directory can charge per lead is usually a fraction of the advertiser's paid-channel CPL. For example, if Google LSA costs a plumber about $53, a directory lead would need to be priced well below that unless it is exclusive.
- The difference between lead-gen and rank-and-rent: a lead-gen directory sells many shared or exclusive leads per category and city, while rank-and-rent rents one site exclusively to one client. Rank-and-rent gives stable income but carries risk if the client churns. Directories spread risk across many businesses.
- These pay-per-lead and pay-per-call economics are almost entirely US-documented. Pay-per-call networks are US-centric (Ringba, Service Direct). In other countries, selling leads directly to businesses is the more likely route.

### Gaps
- I found no non-US pay-per-lead or pay-per-call prices (UK, DE, ES, LatAm or CIS).
- I did not find Service Direct's own current partner payout schedule.
- Price-per-lead figures for directories specifically, as opposed to advertiser CPL, are missing.

## 3. Display advertising (AdSense, Ezoic, Mediavine/Journey, Raptive) and RPM by country

### Takeaway
Revenue per page or visitor differs severalfold by country. Third-party estimates put AdSense page RPM at roughly $15-50 for the US, $12-40 for the UK, $10-25 for Germany and $2-8 for Brazil and LatAm. Ezoic EPMV is about $12-16 for the US, about $13 for Germany and about $2 for Russia. In Russia, Yandex's ad network (RSYA) pays far less: practitioners cite about 1,000-5,000 RUB/month per 1,000 daily visitors. Premium networks gate entry by tier-1 traffic share. Raptive cut its threshold to 25k pageviews in Oct 2025, and Journey by Mediavine needs about 1k sessions.

### Cited Findings
**Thresholds**
- Raptive dropped its requirement from 100,000 to **25,000 monthly pageviews** (Oct 2025). — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/raptive-drops-pageview-requirement-to-25-000-monthly-visits/)
- A user post (unverified) says Raptive's 25k-99k tier requires **50%+ traffic from US/UK/CA/AU/NZ**, falling to 40% at 100k+. — [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rw2u5j/snapshots/2026-03-17T20%3A20%3A18.932354Z)
- Journey by Mediavine requires **1,000 monthly sessions**, plus 30 days of Grow by Mediavine installed. The main Mediavine network requires **$5,000/year in ad revenue**, a revenue threshold rather than a traffic one. — [Productive Blogging (2026)](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/); [Jupiter, Mediavine requirements 2026](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify)
- Mediavine and Journey want majority or "sufficient premium" traffic from US/UK/CA/AU(/NZ). — [MonetizeMore Mediavine review](https://www.monetizemore.com/blog/mediavine-review/)

**AdSense RPM by country (third-party estimates, not official Google data)**
- US **$20-50**, UK **$15-40**, Germany **$10-25** and South America **$2-6** (one blog). Another source gives US $15-40+ and UK $12-30. Brazil is "improving", at **$3-8**. — [Techconda AdSense RPM benchmarks (Feb 2026)](https://www.techconda.com/2026/02/adsense-rpm-benchmarks.html); [Dicloak AdSense RPM by country 2025](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/google-adsense-rpm-by-country-2025-how-to-maximize-revenue-across-countries); [Ranktracker geographic variations](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/geographic-variations-in-cpc-and-rpm-for-adsense/) (attribution approximate; the sources conflict)
- Publisher-side AdSense CPC estimates: Russia about **$0.15**, Spain about **$0.19**. — [World Population Review, AdSense CPC by country 2026](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/adsense-cpc-rates-by-country) (attribution approximate)
- YouTube creator RPM, a different metric that indicates country ratios: US $5.10, UK $4.47, Spain $1.46, Germany $1.34. — [Dynamoi YouTube RPM by country 2026](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm)

**Ezoic EPMV (earnings per 1,000 visitors)**
- US **$11.86** (2026 review) or **$16.31** (repmv chart). Switzerland is $19.73, Canada $18.25, Germany **$13.33** and Russia **$2.24**. The data is undated and up to about 1,000 days old, possibly 2023-24. — [theworldofonlineearnings Ezoic review (Aug 2026)](https://www.theworldofonlineearnings.com/2026/08/ezoic-review.html); [repmv geo EPMV](https://www.repmv.com/en/blog-ezoic-geo-location-epmv); [ybierling CPM by country](https://www.ybierling.com/en/blog-web-cpm-rates-by-country)
- An unofficial calculator site says the network average EPMV is about **$13**, and finance or tech sites reach $20-40. — [ultimatefinancecalculator Ezoic](https://www.ultimatefinancecalculator.com/calculators/ezoic-earnings-calculator/)

**Mediavine/Raptive geo gap**
- Mediavine officially confirms that US advertiser spend is much higher than in other regions, and points publishers to a dashboard Countries breakdown. It gives no number. — [Mediavine help center](https://help.mediavine.com/en/articles/8129488-how-to-find-popular-posts-from-specific-countries-in-ga4)
- An unverified figure: UK/CA/AU traffic earns about **47% of US** Mediavine RPM, and European traffic about **32%**. — [organicarbitrage (Mar 2026)](https://organicarbitrage.pages.dev/articles/mediavine-vs-adthrive-vs-raptive)
- A Journey community thread discusses low RPMs for European visitors. — [Journey community](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/33268340939163-RPM-for-european-visitors)
- An r/juststart thread on RPM/CPM by country (Feb 2024). — [r/juststart mirror](https://lr.us.psf.lt/r/juststart/comments/1b0kkow/rpm_cpm_by_country)

**Russia/CIS: Yandex Advertising Network (RSYA)**
- Practitioner estimates: about **1,000-1,500 RUB/month per 1,000 visitors/day**. Auto and real-estate niches reach about **5,000 RUB/month**, while IT and games pay little. Claimed RPM per 1,000 impressions: IT 300-500 RUB, finance 400-700 RUB, entertainment 100-200 RUB. Most of this data is undated (2023-24) or self-reported. — [shadani.ru](https://shadani.ru/blog/sozdanie-sajtov/monetizacija-sajtov/skolko-zarabatyvaet-veb-sajt-s-poseshhaemostju-1000-chelovek-v-sutki/); [partnerkin (2026)](https://partnerkin.com/blog/articles/zarabotok-s-1000-users); [monetizaci.ru](http://monetizaci.ru/Optimization-Secrets.php)
- A webmaster reports earning about 0.08 RUB per unique visitor, and 0.14-0.17 RUB on construction-niche sites. One case says monthly revenue fell from 45,000 RUB on AdSense to 3,000 RUB on RSYA at 70k uniques/month. Anecdotal. — [shadani.ru](https://shadani.ru/blog/sozdanie-sajtov/monetizacija-sajtov/skolko-zarabatyvaet-veb-sajt-s-poseshhaemostju-1000-chelovek-v-sutki/); [qna.habr.com](https://qna.habr.com/q/749185) (attribution approximate)

### Inferences
- Rough ratios of display revenue per visitor, relative to the US = 1: UK about 0.5-0.8, Germany about 0.4-0.8, Spain about 0.2-0.4 (inferred from YouTube ratios and CPC), LatAm about 0.1-0.2, and Russia via Ezoic or RSYA about 0.1-0.15. At 1 USD ≈ 80-95 RUB, 1,500 RUB/month per 30k visits means about $0.5-0.6 per 1,000 visitors. Russian display revenue is therefore about 20-30x below US levels.
- Directories have low pages per session and transactional intent, so display ads are a secondary revenue stream for them. Display ads also compete with featured listings for attention.
- In non-tier-1 markets, premium networks (Raptive, Mediavine) are effectively closed, because they require a majority of traffic from US/UK/CA/AU/NZ. That leaves AdSense, Ezoic and RSYA as the options.

### Gaps
- I found no official per-country RPM table from Google, Ezoic, Mediavine or Raptive.
- Spain, Mexico and Argentina RPMs for websites are not directly sourced.
- I found no 2025-26 RSYA data that comes from Yandex itself.

## 4. Search ad CPC by country and industry (proxy for the commercial value of traffic)

### Takeaway
US Google Ads CPCs average about $5.26-5.42 across industries (WordStream/LocaliQ 2025/2026), with legal at about $8.58 and travel at about $2.14. Across countries, the US is far above the UK, which is above Spain, Germany and Mexico. One dataset (Dec 2024) gives US $7.66, UK $3.47, Spain $1.65, Germany $1.04 and Mexico $0.66. WordStream puts Germany 31% below the US and Spain and Mexico about 50% below. I found no reliable Google Ads CPC figure for Russia.

### Cited Findings
- WordStream/LocaliQ 2025: all-industry average CPC **$5.26** (up from $4.66 in 2024). Some say this figure is a median. — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/rundowns/where-to-spend-what-to-skip-marketing-moves-that-generate-higher-roi); [WordStream 2025 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks); [christopholivierconsulting](https://christopholivierconsulting.com/?p=5961)
- 2026 edition (Apr 2025-Mar 2026): CPC **$5.42**, CVR **8.18%**, and **87% of industries** saw CPC increases. Travel CPC was $2.14. — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/); [christopholivierconsulting](https://christopholivierconsulting.com/?p=5961)
- By industry (2025): Attorneys & Legal **$8.58** (highest), Home & Home Improvement **$6.55**, Restaurants & Food **$2.05** and Arts & Entertainment **$1.60** (lowest). — [Webtonic 2026 guide](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-complete-pricing-guide-2026); [pipeline-marketing contractors stats](https://pipeline-marketing.pages.dev/blog/google-ads-for-contractors-statistics/); [shno CPC statistics](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/cost-per-click-statistics)
- By country, from Adbenchly (Statista/Semrush, Dec 2024): **US $7.66, UK $3.47, Spain $1.65, Germany $1.04, Mexico $0.66**. I could not fetch the page directly (DNS blocked). — [Adbenchly country CPC](https://adbenchly.com/country/)
- AgencyAnalytics (Feb 2025): US $2.64, UK $1.30, Germany $1.10. The method differs from Adbenchly's. — [AgencyAnalytics](https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/google-ads-cost)
- WordStream's relative figures: Germany is about **31% below** the US average, and Spain and Mexico are each about **50% below**. These are older data. — [WordStream, average CPC by country](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/average-cost-per-click)
- UK average CPC is **£3.42** (240 accounts, Mar 2025-Feb 2026), up from £2.18 in 2022. — [Pace Ads, CPC by country 2026](https://paceads.com/blog/google-ads-cpc-by-country-2026) (attribution approximate; the data is attributed to Visionary Marketing)
- European Google Ads cost guide. — [codedesign.org](https://codedesign.org/google-ads-cost-europe-your-complete-guide)

### Inferences
- The CPC sources disagree up to threefold, but their order is consistent: US > UK > ES ≈ DE ≈ FR > MX/LatAm > RU/CIS. The low German figures in Adbenchly look anomalous given Germany's high RPM and EPMV, which may reflect a different keyword mix.
- In Russia, the commercial value of search traffic is set mainly by Yandex Direct, not Google Ads, so Google CPC figures for Russia are not meaningful.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable Google Ads or Yandex Direct CPC for Russia, Kazakhstan or other CIS countries, and no country-by-industry breakdown outside the US.

## 5. Affiliate programs relevant to directories

### Takeaway
Travel and booking directories can use Viator and GetYourGuide (about 8% of booking value) and Booking.com. Reportedly, from June 2025 Booking.com runs through CJ at 4% on accommodation, 6% on cars and 4% on attractions. Amazon Associates runs separate programs per country. In the EU, a June 2025 change pays category rates (for example about 10-12% on fashion in France) even on indirect purchases.

### Cited Findings
- Booking.com: from June 2025, applicants are routed to CJ, where the stated rates are **4% on accommodation, 6% on car rentals and 4% on attractions**. This is one blog's reading of CJ's page. Older sources describe **25-40% of Booking's own commission**, which works out to low single digits of the booking value. — [track360 travel affiliate rate-card 2026](https://track360.io/blog/best-travel-affiliate-programs-2026-operator-rate-card-benchmark); [creator-hero Booking review](https://creator-hero.com/blog/booking-com-affiliate-program-in-depth-review-pros-and-cons); [makeinfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/booking-coms-affiliate-program-how-travel-creators-earn-commission)
- Viator pays **8% per completed booking**, and GetYourGuide pays **8% base** (variable). — [track360](https://track360.io/blog/best-travel-affiliate-programs-2026-operator-rate-card-benchmark); [HostAdvice travel affiliates 2026](https://hostadvice.com/blog/monetization/affiliate-marketing/best-travel-affiliate-programs/)
- Amazon Associates: each country has its own program and rate card. The UK reaches up to about 12%, varying by category. On **June 2, 2025** the EU changed its rules: indirect purchases used to earn a flat 1% and now earn the purchased product's category rate. France pays 10% on Amazon Fashion, 12% on clothing, shoes and jewelry, and 0% on gift cards. — [Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/?p=2380); [Geniuslink newsletter](https://geniuslink.kit.com/posts/amazon-big-news-conversions-tip); [Cuelinks Amazon rates guide](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-guide/); [Lasso 2026 operator guide](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/)

### Inferences
- Affiliate income suits travel and things-to-do directories (bookings) and tools directories, where SaaS affiliate programs often pay recurring commission (not researched here). It suits local-services directories poorly.
- Booking.com's move to CJ and its effective rate of about 4% make hotel directories less lucrative than tours and activities directories, which earn 8% on full booking value.

### Gaps
- I found no current Amazon category tables for Germany, Spain, Mexico or Brazil.
- I found no SaaS affiliate commission benchmarks (commonly 20-30% recurring), so that range is unverified here.
- I found no CIS affiliate networks (for example Admitad or Ozon/WB affiliates) and no rates for them.

## 6. Sponsorships, paid submissions, job boards, data sales, selling the site

### Takeaway
B2B, SaaS and AI-tool directories earn mainly from paid submissions ($30-70 one-time is common for AI directories, plus featured/sponsor slots). Job boards charge about $50-600 per post. Directories sell at about 20-60x monthly net profit on Empire Flippers, with a typical average of about 31-33x. Small AI directories sell much lower, for example $20k for $1.5-2.6k/month of revenue.

### Cited Findings
- AI-tool directories: AI Tools FYI charges **$30 one-time** for fast-track submission, and AIPURE charges **$69.90 one-time** for paid submission. There's An AI For That uses a paid "Get Featured" form, with no price found. — [nicklaunches, aitools.fyi](https://nicklaunches.com/resources/ai-directories/aitools-fyi); [nicklaunches, AIPURE](https://nicklaunches.com/resources/ai-directories/aipure); [nicklaunches, TAAFT](https://nicklaunches.com/resources/ai-directories/theres-an-ai-for-that)
- A Flippa listing describes an AI tools directory with **$1.5-2.6k/month revenue** from submission packages, guest posts and homepage promotions. ## 7. Which monetization fits which niche type (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Local services, especially in the US, fit lead-gen, pay-per-call, rank-and-rent and featured listings. B2B, SaaS and AI tools fit paid submissions, featured slots, sponsors and affiliate. Travel and attractions fit booking affiliates (8%) and display ads. Job-related niches fit pay-per-post. In non-tier-1 markets (Spain, LatAm, Russia), display income drops 3-20x, so direct sales to businesses become relatively more important.

### Cited Findings
- Local services: home services and legal have the highest CPCs ($6.55 and $8.58) and the highest pay-per-call demand. — [pipeline-marketing](https://pipeline-marketing.pages.dev/blog/google-ads-for-contractors-statistics/); [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-complete-pricing-guide-2026); [Ringba](https://www.ringba.com/blog/the-pay-per-call-report-september-3-2021)
- Tools and software directories: OpenAlternative earns about 35% of revenue from $197/mo featured listings. AI directories charge $30-70 for submissions. — [Dirstarter](https://dirstarter.com/blog/directory-revenue-models); [nicklaunches AIPURE](https://nicklaunches.com/resources/ai-directories/aipure)
- Travel: Viator and GetYourGuide pay 8%, Booking.com about 4% via CJ, and travel CPC is low ($2.14), which implies lower display value per visitor. — [track360](https://track360.io/blog/best-travel-affiliate-programs-2026-operator-rate-card-benchmark); [christopholivierconsulting](https://christopholivierconsulting.com/?p=5961)
- Local classifieds and directories: a $25K MRR case gets most revenue from featured/bump fees and subscriptions, and only 5% from directory placements. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/five-years-in-how-a-boring-local-classifieds-site-quietly-crossed-25k-mrr-4d2395fb81)

### Inferences
- Geo strategy:
  - The US, UK, CA and AU support every method, including premium ad networks and US pay-per-call.
  - In Germany, display ads pay relatively well (Ezoic about $13 EPMV), but these sources do not cover lead-gen pricing.
  - Spain and LatAm have about half the US CPC or lower and low RPM. Featured listings priced in local currency (likely lower than US prices) and affiliate income would carry more of the revenue.
  - In Russia and the CIS, Google premium networks are unavailable and RSYA pays a fraction of US rates. Direct B2B sales (featured placement, leads) are the main lever, alongside Yandex-based ads.

### Gaps
- I found no study comparing directory monetization mixes across countries. The geo guidance above is inferred from ad-market data, not from directory operators.
