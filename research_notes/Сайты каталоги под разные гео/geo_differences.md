# Geo differences for niche directory websites (USA/UK, EU/Spain, LatAm, Russia/CIS)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Method: WebSearch only. WebFetch to aepd.es failed (DNS blocked in this environment), so no primary pages were read in full, and every finding comes from search snippets or summaries. Older or conflicting data is flagged inline. **This is not legal advice.** Every legal point below needs a check by a local lawyer before you act on it.

## 1. Search ecosystem & competition (Google/Yandex share, local aggregators, incumbents)

### Takeaway
Google dominates search in the US/UK, the EU and LatAm, at roughly 88–96% depending on source and date. Russia is the exception: Yandex has about 70–75% and Google about 22–28%. A Russian directory therefore has to rank in Yandex, which runs a different algorithm and puts more weight on behavioral and commercial factors. It also competes with strong local platforms: 2GIS, Yandex Maps, Avito and Profi.ru. In Spain and LatAm, vertical incumbents are strong (Doctoralia/Docplanner, Milanuncios, Páginas Amarillas). That means horizontal "yellow pages" style directories are crowded, while narrow niches are less so.

## 2. Legal constraints per region

### Takeaway
The EU, and Spain especially, is the most restrictive geo for scraping and contacting businesses. Sole traders (autónomos) are personal data. The AEPD issued a criterion in May 2025 and sanctioned business-info companies (Informa, Iberinform, Datacentric) for repackaging autónomo data without a valid legal basis. Cold B2B email falls under LSSI art. 21 and needs prior consent unless there is an existing client relationship. Germany adds a mandatory Impressum, with fines up to €50k plus competitor Abmahnungen, and a litigious environment for review sites. The DSA applies to any hosting of user content. The US is the most permissive: CAN-SPAM is opt-out, CCPA has thresholds, and Section 230 protects user reviews. Russia brings data localization (152-FZ, tightened in 2025), mandatory ad marking (ORD/erid) plus a 3% ad levy, and loss of Google AdSense.

### Cited Findings
**Spain / GDPR**
- On 16 May 2025 the AEPD published a legal criterion on the legal basis and information duties when processing data of autónomos. The related resolutions say third parties cannot use autónomo data from the census to build business-information products — [AEPD criterio jurídico](https://www.aepd.es/informes-y-resoluciones/criterios-juridicos-aepd/tratamiento-datos-personales-empresarios-autonomos); [Fieldfisher analysis](https://www.fieldfisher.com/es-es/locations/spain/insights/bases-datos-empresariales)
- In May 2025 the AEPD issued resolutions against Informa, Iberinform Internacional and Datacentric, ordering them to stop processing until they have a valid legal basis — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/es-es/locations/spain/insights/bases-datos-empresariales)
- Art. 19 LOPDGDD recognizes processing of contact data and data of sole traders for professional purposes, but within the general GDPR framework. Per Fieldfisher, recent AEPD resolutions hold that marketing use of autónomo data cannot rely on legitimate interest except under the very restrictive conditions of art. 21.2 GDPR — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/es-es/locations/spain/insights/bases-datos-empresariales). Industry data vendors still invoke legitimate interest with "public sources," which contrasts with the AEPD position — [Axesor](https://www.axesor.es/explicaciones/bases-datos-marketing/condiciones-autonomos-profesionales)
- Cold email: commercial email is governed by LSSI art. 21, which prevails over LOPDGDD. Prior express consent is the general rule even in B2B. The only exception is a prior contractual relationship. Tacit consent is not accepted, and an opt-out is required in every message — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/es-es/locations/spain/insights/bases-datos-empresariales); [AEPD report 2018-0164](https://www.aepd.es/es/documento/2018-0164.pdf); [Genbeta on AEPD fines](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/que-te-envien-correos-publicidad-que-te-dejen-rechazarlos-puede-salir-caro-a-quien-envia-aepd-multa). One vendor guide (Overloop 2026) claims legitimate interest is accepted for corporate role addresses. That conflicts with Fieldfisher, so treat it with caution — [Overloop](https://overloop.com/blog/es/b2b-cold-email-espana-rgpd-aepd)

**Germany**
- The Impressum duty has been in § 5 DDG since 16 May 2024, replacing the TMG, with substantively the same content. It applies to any "geschäftsmäßig" online offer. Fines go up to €50,000 (§ 33 DDG), and competitors can also send an Abmahnung under the UWG — [IHK Rheinhessen](https://www.ihk.de/rheinhessen/rechtundsteuern/internetrecht/impressum/impressumspflicht-1449704); [IHK Aachen](https://www.ihk.de/aachen/recht/rechtsinformationen/aktuelle-dokumente-zum-thema-recht/impressumspflicht-im-internet-607352); [IHK Potsdam](https://www.ihk.de/potsdam/ihk-service-und-beratung/recht-merkblaetter/neuer-inhalt170606-korrektes-impressum-erstellen-3752902). Journalistic-editorial sites also need a responsible person under § 18 MStV — [NLM Leitfaden 2024](https://www.nlm.de/fileadmin/dateien/pdf/leitfaden_impressumspflicht_2024.pdf)

**EU Digital Services Act**
- Art. 19 exempts micro and small enterprises (Recommendation 2003/361/EC) from the online-platform obligations in Arts. 20–28. The exemption does not cover hosting-level duties: Art. 16 notice-and-action and Art. 17 statements of reasons still apply to any directory hosting user content (reviews, user-submitted listings). It does not apply to VLOPs — [presencis DSA Art. 19](https://presencis.com/regulations/dsa/article-19/); [sota.io DSA guide](https://sota.io/blog/eu-digital-services-act-2024-hosting-provider-developer-guide). Sources partly conflict on scope, so verify against the OJ text.

**USA**
- CAN-SPAM has no B2B exemption and applies even to a single individually addressed sales email. The maximum civil penalty is reported as $53,088 per email after the Jan 2025 inflation adjustment (vendor sources; the FTC's archived guide shows an outdated $16,000) — [prospeo CAN-SPAM penalties](https://prospeo.io/s/can-spam-penalties); [FTC guide (archived)](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20130214054537/http://business.ftc.gov/documents/bus61-can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business). The regime is opt-out: cold email is allowed if it carries no deceptive headers, has a physical address and honors unsubscribes.
- CCPA applies if gross revenue exceeds $26.625M (from 1 Jan 2025), or the business buys/sells/shares data of 100k+ CA consumers or households, or derives 50%+ of revenue from selling or sharing data — [Orrick InfoBytes](https://infobytes.orrick.com/2024-12-20/california-privacy-protection-agency-increases-fines-and-penalties-for-2025/); [clym](https://www.clym.io/blog/ccpa-applicability-guide)
- Section 230 (immunity for third-party content such as user reviews) was not verified in this session's searches. See Gaps.

**Russia**
- 152-FZ localization: Law No. 23-FZ (signed 28 Feb 2025, effective 1 Jul 2025) extended the localization duty to anyone processing on behalf of an operator and added a duty to tell Roskomnadzor where databases are located. Roskomnadzor must be notified before processing starts, and a missing notification has been a standalone offense since 30 May 2025 (RUB 30–50k). Sources conflict on fines: localization fines of RUB 1–6M for a first offense and 6–18M for repeats, with claims of turnover-based fines up to 3% for repeat leaks (unconfirmed) — [B1 Law Messenger](https://b1.ru/en/insights/law-messenger/localization-of-personal-data-of-russian-citizens-6-march-2025/); [Konsu group](https://konsugroup.com/en/news/new-requirements-personal-data-protection-russia-2025-07/); [h-cons](https://h-cons.ru/uslugi-yuridicheskim-licam/pravovoj-audit-sajta/uvedomlenie-rkn-o-rabote-s-persdannymi); [PGP law](https://pgplaw.com/analytics-and-brochures/alerts/gotovitsya-zakonoproekt-o-vvedenii-novykh-i-ob-uvelichenii-deystvuyushchikh-shtrafov-za-nesoblyudeni/)
- Ad marking (ОРД/ERIR): every online ad aimed at Russian users, paid or free, must carry the label "реклама", advertiser details and an erid token, and must be reported via an ORD (VK, Yandex ORD, Ozon ORD, MediaScout, First ORD, etc.) to the state register ERIR. From 1 Jan 2025 the KKTU classifier is required. Social ads need marking from 1 Mar 2025. A 3% levy on online-ad revenue applies from 1 Apr 2025 (Art. 18.2 of the Advertising Law) and also covers intermediaries. Fines date from 2023 (274-FZ). This applies directly to paid/featured listings and banners sold by a directory — [Yandex Adv news](https://yandex.com/adv/news/how-mandatory-online-advertising-labeling-works-in-2025); [russia-promo](https://russia-promo.com/blog/understanding-ad-labeling-in-russia); [Gorodissky](https://www.gorodissky.com/publications/articles/recent-developments-in-regulation-of-online-advertising-in-russia/); [workspace.ru guide 2026](https://workspace.ru/blog/polnyy-gayd-po-markirovke-reklamy-v-2026-godu/); [Yandex partner marking](https://yandex.ru/support/affiliate-plus/ru/mark)
- **Correction to the brief:** AdSense did not stop paying Russia-based publishers in 2022. In March 2022 Google stopped serving ads to users in Russia. In August 2024 it deactivated all Russia-based AdSense accounts, with final payouts on 21/26 Aug 2024 — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/google/google-deactivates-russian-adsense-accounts-sends-final-payments/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-halts-adsense-monetization-for-russia-based-publishers/524212/); [SERoundtable](https://seroundtable.com/google-deactivates-russia-based-adsense-accounts-37889.html)

### Inferences
- Scraping Google Maps or registries for autónomos in Spain, then emailing them to sell listings, is the highest-risk pattern found in this research. A safer approach: list businesses from public sources with minimal data, give an easy opt-out, comply with art. 14 information duties, and sell via phone or inbound rather than cold email. A lawyer is needed here.
- A German site needs an Impressum with a real address. That makes anonymous operation impractical.
- A Russian directory selling featured placements must run an ORD workflow and pay the 3% levy. Hosting user data needs Russian servers plus Roskomnadzor notification. A foreign operator also faces sanctions and payment barriers.

### Gaps
- Section 230 applied to directories/reviews, and UK GDPR/PECR (UK B2B email to corporate subscribers is softer than Spain's). Not searched. From background knowledge: PECR allows unsolicited B2B email to corporate subscribers but not to sole traders or partnerships. Verify.
- The full text of the AEPD May 2025 criterion. The page was blocked (aepd.es needs to be allowed in network settings to fetch it).
- France/Italy specifics (CNIL B2B email rules, Italian Garante) were not researched.

### Takeaway
I did not find dedicated sources in this session. The notes below are background knowledge and must be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Docplanner shows the multi-country pattern: one platform with per-country brands and domains (ZnanyLekarz.pl, Doctoralia.es / .com.mx / .com.br, MioDottore.it, Jameda.de) — [docplanner.com](https://docplanner.com/); [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/la-espanola-doctoralia-se-une-a-docplaner-para-formar-una-de-las-mayores-plataformas-de-salud-de-europa)

### Inferences
- Spain: Spanish-only is usually enough for an MVP. Catalan versions (with hreflang es-ES / ca-ES) may help for Catalonia-heavy niches. Basque and Galician have smaller search demand. Unverified, so check search volume via DataForSEO.
- Spanish across LatAm needs per-country variants (es-MX, es-AR, es-CO) with local vocabulary and currency. Brazil needs pt-BR.
- ccTLDs (.es, .de, .com.mx) give a local trust signal. A .com with subfolders (/es/, /mx/) concentrates authority. Both work with correct hreflang. This is a general SEO consensus, not sourced here.

### Gaps

## 4. Monetization (RPM/CPC, willingness to pay, payment methods, РСЯ)

### Takeaway
Ad revenue per visitor falls steeply from the US/UK to Germany, Spain and LatAm. Russia has no AdSense and relies on the Yandex Advertising Network (РСЯ), where webmasters report falling partner revenue since late 2023. Local payment rails matter for selling listings to small businesses: Bizum in Spain (about 30.6M users), Pix in Brazil (42% of e-commerce value in 2025) and СБП in Russia.

### Cited Findings
- Ad revenue by country follows a consistent ordering, US > UK > DE > ES > MX, but absolute website AdSense RPM figures in blogs are unreliable. Examples: US $20–50, DE $10–25, ES $4–12. Spain CPC is about $0.19 and Mexico about $0.14 (older table) — [ranktracker geo variations](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/geographic-variations-in-cpc-and-rpm-for-adsense); [thesrzone](https://www.thesrzone.com/2022/03/adsense-cpc-rates-by-country-2022.html); [dicloak](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/google-adsense-rpm-by-country-2025-how-to-maximize-revenue-across-countries)
- A better-measured proxy is YouTube creator RPM (Nov 2025 to Nov 2026, Dynamoi): US $5.11, UK $4.50, Spain $1.46, Mexico $0.26. That puts Spain at about 3.5x lower and Mexico at about 20x lower than the US — [Dynamoi US](https://dynamoi.com/es/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/us); [Dynamoi ES](https://dynamoi.com/nl/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/es); [Dynamoi MX](https://dynamoi.com/es/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/mx)
- РСЯ: webmasters report mass cuts to partner revenue since Oct 2023, with income falling 2–3x for some (opinion pieces). Partnerkin published a May 2026 piece on falling РСЯ revenue by niche (figures not seen) — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/1028574-rsya-prodolzhaet-grabit-vebmasterov); [partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/blog/stati/context_reduction); [Yandex РСЯ help](https://yandex.ru/support/partner/ru/)
- Bizum: about 30.6M users at the end of 2025 (+13.2% YoY), about €67.7bn volume. Online: 105.6M purchases, €5.4bn, 111k merchants. About 32M users by mid-2026 — [financer.es](https://financer.es/finanzas/estadisticas-bizum-espana/); [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2026/01/19/el-volumen-de-operaciones-con-bizum-crecio-un-53-en-2025-y-se-situo-en-67751-millones/); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/05/09/spains-bizum-heads-to-the-high-street-to-take-on-visa-and-mastercard); [Stripe Bizum guide](https://stripe.com/de/resources/more/bizum-in-depth-guide)
- Pix: 42% of Brazilian e-commerce value in 2025, against 40% for credit cards (Global Payments Report 2026). Ebanx forecasts 45% by end-2026 and 50% by 2028. In Mexico, A2A is 6% of e-commerce and wallets 26% — [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/pix-chega-a-42-dos-pagamentos-on-line-no-brasil/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3470758); [Let's Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/pix/pix-lidera-conta-a-conta-america-latina/)
- Russia: СБП volume exceeded RUB 100 trillion in 2025 (CBR), and 73% of СБП payments were online (Russian Standard acquiring). ЮKassa merchant turnover grew 26% — [CBR results 2025](https://www.cbr.ru/about_br/publ/results_work/2025/razvitie-sistemy-platezhey-i-raschetov/); [eanews](https://eanews.ru/rossiya/20251009163903/sbp-qr-kody-i-biometriya-zaymut-50-onlayn-tranzaktsiy-v-rossii-k-sleduyuschemu-godu); [retail.ru](https://www.retail.ru/news/po-itogam-2025-goda-e-commerce-v-rossii-vyros-na-26-26-yanvarya-2026-273777)
- Doctoralia monetizes through paid subscriptions for professionals (freemium listings plus paid plans), which shows that SMBs in ES and LatAm will pay for visibility and bookings in high-value verticals — [zoftwarehub Doctoralia plans](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/doctoralia/plans)

### Inferences
- With the same traffic, display ads in Spain earn roughly 3–4x less than in the US, and LatAm earns far less again. In ES and LatAm, directories should lean on listing fees and lead sales instead of AdSense.
- A non-Russian operator would struggle to collect RUB payments. ЮKassa/СБП require a Russian legal entity or sole proprietor (background knowledge, unverified).
- SEPA Direct Debit plus Stripe (cards, Bizum via Redsys/Stripe) is the practical stack for recurring listing subscriptions in Spain.

### Gaps
- Google Ads CPC benchmarks for local services by country (e.g., "fontanero Madrid" vs "plumber NYC"). Pulling these from DataForSEO is recommended.
- Survey data on SMB willingness to pay for listings by country. None found.
- Mexico payment methods (OXXO, Mercado Pago, SPEI) were not covered.

## 5. Geo-arbitrage / multi-country directory examples

### Takeaway
The best documented example is Docplanner/Doctoralia: one shared platform behind country brands in 13 markets across the EU, Turkey and LatAm. It is a proof that a directory concept can be "cloned" across geos with localization. It also shows that a well-funded incumbent can occupy the same niche everywhere.

### Cited Findings
- Docplanner lists 13 markets, and the 2016 merger with Barcelona-based Doctoralia gave it 25 countries at the time. Figures since then vary (13, 20, 25) — [docplanner.com](https://docplanner.com/); [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/la-espanola-doctoralia-se-une-a-docplaner-para-formar-una-de-las-mayores-plataformas-de-salud-de-europa); [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/rendez-vous-medicaux-docplanner-leve-20-millions-de-dollars-et-fusionne-avec-doctoralia/245980)
- Docplanner raised €15M to expand in LatAm (FrenchWeb) — [FrenchWeb LatAm](https://www.frenchweb.fr/docplanner-leve-15-millions-deuros-pour-setendre-en-amerique-latine/293677)

### Inferences
- Before translating a US niche directory concept into Spanish, check whether a Docplanner-style roll-up or a local classified site (Milanuncios, Avito) already owns that niche.

### Gaps
- Habitissimo, Cronoshare, Top Doctors and Profi.ru expansion histories returned no results. Examples of small or indie niche directories that succeeded in multiple countries were not found.

## 6. Researching demand per geo

### Takeaway
