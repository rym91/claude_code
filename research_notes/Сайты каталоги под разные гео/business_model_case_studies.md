# Niche Directory Websites: Business Model, Case Studies, Niche Selection

Research date: 2026-11-09. Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch to sidehustlenation.com failed with a DNS block, so no full-page reads). Most numbers below come from search-result snippets of podcasts, vendor blogs and summary sites. **Almost all revenue figures are self-reported, often by people who sell directory courses, templates or SaaS (Frey Chu: Ship Your Directory + Enrich.directory; Dirstarter, Directorist, HivePress, Jasmine Directory, Stormy AI are vendors).** No audited numbers were found.

## 1. Documented case studies (niche, geo, traffic, revenue, monetization, time, cost)

### Takeaway
The best-documented cases are small: Frey Chu's portfolio earns roughly $2–2.5K/month (self-reported, 2025), and his first thrifting directory reached ~1,000 visitors/day about 6 months after launch. The big numbers (Cursor Directory ~$35K/mo, Remote OK ~$3M/yr, Sober Nation $250K profit) come from B2B/tech or high-LTV lead-gen niches. Several headline figures ($10.5K/mo, $350K/yr, $273/day) appear only in episode or video titles and don't match the content.

### Cited Findings

**Frey Chu ("The Directory Guy", US local directories, WordPress + GeoDirectory)**
- His first directory launched in 2022, when he was "just learning SEO", and was in the thrifting niche. His other niches include porta-potty rentals, flea markets, liquidation stores and a national healthcare directory called Discover Plasma. — [WP GeoDirectory founder story](https://wpgeodirectory.com/founder-story-frey-chou/)
- He found the thrifting directory was getting about 1,000 visitors/day six months after launch, and it made about $1,200 in its first month of ads. — [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/online-directory/) (via search snippet)
- He says the thrifting directory has brought in "a couple thousand dollars a month passively for about two years". He won't share the URL because "I've had a few copycats". — [WP GeoDirectory](https://wpgeodirectory.com/founder-story-frey-chou/)
- His portfolio income is put at about $2,500/mo in recurring revenue (Niche Pursuits episode, Aug 27 2025, and Side Hustle Show ep. 692). In one clip he says "last month it did around $2,300". — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/frey-chu-directory-sites/); [Side Hustle Show 692](https://audioboom.com/posts/8766031); [Podscripts transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-startup-ideas-podcast/the-actual-process-behind-a-105kmonth-directory-website)
- Monetization: display ads come first. He uses Mediavine Grow and calls AdSense "the closest thing that I've ever experienced to passive income". Featured listings and software upsells come second. His advice is to get steady traffic before trying monetization. — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/frey-chu-directory-sites/)
- Lead gen: he cites luxury porta-potty and restroom trailers (rentals around $2,000/day) as a high-ticket referral-fee niche. - Build cost: a luxury restroom trailer directory was "fully enriched in four days for under $250" using Claude Code, Outscraper and Enrich.directory, work he says would have taken 2,000+ hours by hand. The episode title claims "$273/Day", which I couldn't verify. — [Spotify episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5XePIUeoqsfluYukPle0eM); [videohighlight summary](https://videohighlight.com/v/I_wbc5ND79o)

### Inferences
- Two different models are lumped together as "directories". (a) Local consumer directories (thrifting, dog parks, porta-potties) earn mostly from display ads, with a realistic ceiling of low thousands per month for a single person, and lead gen is the upside. (b) B2B/tech directories (Cursor, OpenAlternative, job boards) earn from vendors paying for placement and reach $5–35K MRR, but they depend on a timely trend and a community.
- Time to first revenue: about 6 months to meaningful traffic (Frey Chu, thrifting) and about 12 months to meaningful revenue (OpenAlternative). Faster cases rode new trends (Cursor, AI tools).
- Build cost with scraping, AI and WordPress is now under $250 plus hosting. The low cost is exactly why copycats appear quickly (see the restroom trailer niche).

### Gaps
- No traffic or revenue data for named porta-potty, sauna, EV-charger or pickleball directories. Searches for sauna and pickleball case studies returned nothing citable.
- I couldn't verify the "$273/day" and "$10.5K/month" title claims against the episode content.
- Ship Your Directory price not found.

## 2. Niche-selection criteria and tooling

### Takeaway
Practitioners combine keyword data (enough volume, low keyword difficulty, SERPs that are mixed or full of weak sites and outdated directories) with "data moat" enrichment: attributes users care about that Google Maps lacks. Monetization fit (advertisers or high-ticket services) is checked last. The thresholds differ between sources.

### Cited Findings
- Frey Chu reportedly weighs niche selection "70% data, 30% passion". The data side is high search volume, low keyword difficulty, and keywords whose results already show directory-style sites. — [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/online-directory/)
- Volume bands conflict. One source says 10,000–40,000 monthly searches plus an outdated incumbent directory (the "bounty"). The Isenberg episode summary says 30–100K monthly searches with keyword difficulty under 20. — [Side Hustle Nation](https://sidehustlenation.com/frey); [Whatfinger summary](https://startup.whatfinger.com/?p=22728); [Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/blog/near-me-seo-strategy-profitable-directory-niches)
- Fragmented intent: in Frey Chu's words, if Google shows a mix of categories, people are struggling to find what they want, and that is the gap to fill. Small or indie sites already ranking show the niche is winnable. — [WP GeoDirectory](https://wpgeodirectory.com/founder-story-frey-chou/)
- Data enrichment (the moat): base fields are name, address, phone, reviews, hours and Street View. The enrichment layer adds amenities such as shade and benches for dog parks, parsed from reviews and websites with AI. Frey co-built Enrich.directory for this. — [Whatfinger](https://startup.whatfinger.com/?p=22728); [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/frey-chu-directory-sites/); [Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/blog/scalable-data-scraping-ai-directory-rankings)
- Validation: the Dog Park episode covers keyword research, social proof such as Reddit and Facebook groups, and a basic directory structure. — [Spotify episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0sq1G4q8yHqZiGC2UzgySa)
- Vendor guides recommend Google Keyword Planner (free), Ubersuggest, Ahrefs and Google Trends. Their advice: avoid broad "all businesses" directories because Google Maps and Yelp already cover them, check whether you can find enough listings in about an hour, and favor niches where businesses already pay for ads or other directories. — [HivePress](https://hivepress.io/blog/choose-a-niche-for-a-business-directory-website/); [Directorist](https://directorist.com/blog/how-to-choose-niche-for-directory-website/); [Business Directory Plugin](https://businessdirectoryplugin.com/niche-directory/)
- Data tools: Outscraper for Google Maps scraping, Enrich.directory for enrichment, and WordPress with GeoDirectory for the site. — [Outscraper](https://outscraper.com/?p=35900); [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/online-directory/)
- Ad-network thresholds: Mediavine and Raptive (AdThrive) require tens of thousands of pageviews per month (guide estimates 50–100K), with RPMs around $14–40. These are the guide's estimates. — [buildthekeyword](https://buildthekeyword.com/blog?post=how-to-build-a-directory-with-visitors-on-autopilot)

### Inferences
- A workable checklist: (1) head term plus "near me" or city variants with combined volume of about 10–100K and keyword difficulty under about 20; (2) the SERP shows mixed or irrelevant results or a dated directory; (3) 1–10K+ entities can be scraped from Maps; (4) at least 3–5 enrichable attributes not on Maps; (5) monetization path: ad RPM, vendors who already buy ads, or high LTV and ticket size for lead gen.
- No source documented a "businesses without websites" criterion or a specific DataForSEO workflow. Those are inferred practices, not cited ones.

### Gaps
- No primary written source of Frey Chu's exact thresholds.
- No published DataForSEO-based niche-finding workflow from a practitioner.

## 3. Failure modes, realistic expectations, saturation 2025–2026, critics

### Takeaway
Success stories suffer from survivorship bias: builders launch many sites and keep the winners. No source gives a measured failure rate. In 2025–2026 the cheap AI/scraping build created copycats (visible in the restroom trailer niche) and an "AI tools directory" glut. AI Overviews are taking clicks from horizontal directories, while operators claim vertical, enriched directories are holding up.

### Cited Findings
- One builder launched a directory every week, and after a year kept only those with traffic. — [johnrushx substack](https://johnrushx.substack.com/p/anyone-can-do-this-took-me-8h)
- Frey Chu won't reveal his URLs because earlier disclosure attracted copycats. — [WP GeoDirectory](https://wpgeodirectory.com/founder-story-frey-chou/)
- A vendor estimate puts first-year niche directory revenue at $500–5,000/month. This comes from a seller of directory software. — [Dirstarter](https://dirstarter.com/blog/how-much-do-directory-websites-make)
- Critic: the AI-directory craze is called "2000s web directory déjà vu". Most such directories bring little traffic, and the author expects the hype to fade. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/souvic/the-ai-directory-craze-is-a-2000s-web-directory-deja-vu-5e7g)
- In one anecdote, of 50+ directories submitted to, about 40% were dead or broken and about 25% had moved to paid listings. Small sample. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/claudeprime/the-real-ai-directory-list-for-2026-what-actually-works-44pk)
- AI era: one operator reports a median 47% drop (2023–2024) in referral traffic from the top 10 horizontal directories, with vertical directories holding steady. Pew (July 2025) found only about 1% of users click links cited in an AI Overview. — [Entrepreneur.com](https://www.entrepreneur.com/growth-strategies/why-i-rebuilt-a-19-year-old-web-directory-in-the-age-of-ai/503074); [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/the-ai-search-reckoning-is-dismantling-open-web-traffic-and-publishers-may-never-recover)
- AI Overviews cite Reddit threads over directories for personal-experience queries. — [DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/how-directory-sites-survive-ai-era)
- r/bigseo anecdote: removing a client's directory listings led to a 12% organic traffic drop. This suggests directory citations still matter to businesses, which is a selling point for paid listings. — [Medium summary](https://medium.com/@nichesitegrowth/directories-still-have-massive-potential-8f69adaa7f79)
- Frey's own warning is not to monetize before traffic is steady. — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/frey-chu-directory-sites/)

### Inferences
- Main failure modes: no traffic because of weak SERP selection or Google not indexing thin programmatic pages; a copycat or a better-funded incumbent outranks you; ad income is too small (low RPM, below network thresholds); lead gen needs sales effort. AI Overviews and Maps answer "near me" intent directly.
- Plan with a portfolio mindset. Expect a 6–12 month horizon per site, and most sites will earn under $500/mo.

### Gaps
- No credible statistic on what share of directories fail.
- No first-hand r/juststart or r/SideProject threads were retrievable.

## 4. Valuations and sales

### Takeaway
Content and directory sites trade at roughly 20–50x monthly profit (about 2–4x annual). Flippa multiples run below broker multiples. No directory-specific multiple dataset exists.

### Cited Findings
- Empire Flippers: multiples are reported at about 20–50x monthly profit, averaging about 31x TTM. Commission is about 15% up to $700K, with an exclusivity period. These come from affiliate review blogs. — [nateshivar](https://www.nateshivar.com/?p=22394); [Empire Flippers valuation tool](https://valuationtool.empireflippers.com/)
- Flippa multiples tend to be lower than brokers' (Empire Flippers, Motion Invest), and listings are not vetted. — [thewebsiteflip Flippa review](https://thewebsiteflip.com/review/flippa/)
- Examples: a 10-year-old US business directory on Flippa (established 2013, price undisclosed, reserve met), and a 7-year-old real estate directory with a $45K asking price (April 2025). — [Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/11509361-a-10-year-old-profitable-and-easy-to-run-online-u-s-business-directory?locale=en); [thewebsiteflip deal flow](https://thewebsiteflip.com/analysis/deal-flow/apr-9-2025/)

### Inferences
- At $2K/mo profit and about 30x, a directory is worth about $60K. Recurring featured-listing revenue likely values higher than volatile ad revenue.

### Gaps
- No directory-specific multiple statistics from Flippa, Empire Flippers or Acquire market reports.
